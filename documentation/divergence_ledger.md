# Divergence ledger

Where xLION's lighting differs from the shipped technique it would otherwise copy. Rule (Tomas, 2026-10-28): default to the shipped
technique (Lumen, Frostbite, EA GIBS surfels, RTXDI, AMD FidelityFX SSSR / denoiser, ...); diverge only with evidence - the game-sequence
benchmark (p95 / max) and before/after shots of real views - that we beat the reference, or revert. One entry per divergence, kept short.

| # | Area | Ours | Reference | Why we differ | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GI read: which surfels a pixel blends | Per-mesh read grid: per 3D cell its parts (a wall cuts a floor), per part the surfels in reach that the part's points see (lists of up to 32, corner sight weights), Wendland C2 blend | EA GIBS: a world-space hash grid of surfels, every surfel whose radius covers the pixel blended by distance and normal; Lumen: the surface cache's cards, sampled bilinearly | Leaks: the grid's parts and sight weights keep a room's light off the next room's floor and the frame behind blinds out of the slats | Leak tests 6a/6n hold; the sight test failed on convex surfaces (2026-10-28: a 25 cm sphere's lists 4.6 entries, Voronoi plates on Police's spheres) - fixed by the chord's sag (SeesOverCurve).
